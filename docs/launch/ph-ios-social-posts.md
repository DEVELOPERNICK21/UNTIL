## Reddit · r/SideProject (text post)

**Title:** UNTIL - day, month, year and life left on your home screen (now on iPhone)

**Body:**

I built UNTIL because clocks and calendars never answered a simple question for me: how much of today, this year, and my life is left?

It’s a time-left app with widgets and Lock Screen surfaces so you see progress without opening another productivity app. Ember is a small companion tied to the day. Android also has Wear OS Day %. There’s a Mac/Windows menu-bar companion that uses the same account.

Stack: React Native, native widgets, Wear OS, Tauri desktop, RevenueCat.

Now live on the App Store (was Android-first). Product Hunt iOS launch is scheduled for 17 Nov (midnight Pacific).

Looking for feedback on:
1. First-open clarity (do people understand “time left” in 10 seconds?)
2. Which widget people actually keep
3. Anything that feels heavy or guilt-y (I want the opposite)
