How much of today is left? Of this year? Of your life?

UNTIL shows you on your Home Screen, Lock Screen and Apple Watch, so you see it every time you pick up your phone, without opening an app.

WIDGETS FOR HOME SCREEN AND LOCK SCREEN
• Day, Month, Year and Life: the percentage and the time that is left
• Deadline: the days left until a date that matters
• Counter: tap to add +1 (water, pages, workouts, anything)
• Hour timer: tap to start and stop
• Tasks: today's list at a glance
• Next deadlines: your nearest three, in order (Premium)

YOUR LIFE IN WEEKS
Set your birth date and see your whole life as a grid of weeks. A calm, plain reminder that time is limited and today counts.

COUNTDOWNS THAT KEEP YOU HONEST
Add an exam, a launch, a trip or a birthday and watch the days go down.

ON YOUR WRIST AND ON THE ISLAND
• Apple Watch: today's % on the watch face, and a Time Hub on your wrist
• Dynamic Island and Live Activity on iPhones that have them (Premium)

MAKE TODAY COUNT
• Daily tasks and tap counters
• Streaks and badges for showing up
• Ember, a small companion that reacts to your day
• Share a clean progress picture
