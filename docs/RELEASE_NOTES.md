## Version 1.0 (Initial release)

### Short (for store listing, ~500 characters)

```
Welcome to UNTIL : Countdown & Time Left. See your time, clearly.

• Day progress: watch the day fill; hours and minutes left in real time
• Month & year: how much of this month and year is already gone
• Life view: your life in progress; set your horizon and see what’s left
• Home screen widgets: day, month, year on your home screen (Android)
• Countdowns to deadlines and important dates
• Custom counters: tap-to-increment for habits (e.g. water, steps)

Minimal design. No clutter. One app for time awareness.
```

### Full

**What’s new in 1.0**

- **Day progress:** Real-time progress through today with hours and minutes passed and remaining.
- **Month & year progress:** See how much of the current month and year has passed.
- **Life view:** Set your expected lifespan and see your life progress and what’s left.
- **Home screen widgets (Android):** Day, month, and year progress widgets for your home screen.
- **Countdowns:** Add countdowns to important dates (e.g. projects, interviews).
- **Custom counters:** Create tap-to-increment counters (e.g. glasses of water, habits) with a home screen widget.
- **Settings:** Set birth date and expected lifespan; data stored locally on your device.
- **Privacy:** No account required; your data stays on your device.

Thank you for using UNTIL : Countdown & Time Left. If you have feedback, reach out via the contact link on our website.
