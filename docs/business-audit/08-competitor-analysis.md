## 2. Competitor by competitor

### 1 · Left: Widgets for Time Left (iOS, Cntxt Limited)
- **Positioning:** a broader "time + habits" toolkit. Subtitle "Countdown, Habits, Streaks".
- **Features:** Time Left (year, month, week, day, hour, life as symbols), habits and streaks, countdowns with to-dos, planner, fasting tool with Live Activity, friends and shared dates, many Home/Lock Screen widgets.
- **Pricing and subscription:** Premium is required to use features (weekly, yearly or lifetime). Price list $4.69–$82 unlabeled.
- **Store presentation:** 258 MB, English plus 29 languages, iOS 26 or later, updated 24 Oct 2026.
- **Reviews:** "takes all your data and then asks you to pay to actually use it" (critical); a buyer says they were charged again after buying lifetime and was told to Restore.
- **Strengths:** breadth, 30 languages, social features, active development.
- **Weaknesses:** hard paywall, bloat, iOS-only, current-iOS-only (26).
- **Opportunity for UNTIL:** trust. "Day and Year free forever", clear renewal text, a calm product with fewer moving parts, and Android.

### 2 · Countdown & Year Progress (Android, Dope Capybara Play)
- **Positioning:** the closest Android rival: productivity utility with life in weeks.
- **Features:** year / month / week / day progress, **20 home screen widgets**, goal tracker, habit tracker with streaks, countdowns, Life in Weeks calendar, offline, no account.
- **Pricing:** free with ads and IAP (amounts not seen). **Presentation:** Productivity, ~13K installs, 4.96★ from 174 ratings, updated 29 Oct 2026.
- **Strengths:** many widgets, high rating, active, free on first use.
- **Weaknesses:** ads, a feature-heavy "tracker" feel rather than an emotional product; no watch or desktop seen.
- **Opportunity:** UNTIL must beat it on *feeling and polish*, not feature count. Compare widgets side by side on a mid-range phone before the next release.

### 3 · LifeWidget – Life Progress (Android, TANT Gold)
- **Positioning:** minimal widgets for life, year, month and week; Material You theming; **free, no ads, no IAP, collects no data** (listing).
- **Presentation:** Productivity, 23K+ installs, 4.0★ from 44 ratings, updated 29 Sept 2026. India-based developer.
- **Strength:** free and private. **Weakness:** thin; no product depth.
- **Implication:** a free life-widget alternative exists on Android with more installs than UNTIL has users `[DATA]`. UNTIL's paid line must be personal value (Life everywhere, style), not "has a Life widget".

### 4 · Year Progress: Life in Motion (iOS, CODEBIT)
- **Positioning:** year / quarter / month / week progress, countdowns, reminders. Lifestyle.
- **Pricing:** Premium $4.99, Remove ads $0.99. English, Japanese, Chinese (2 variants). 4.8★ from 5 ratings; last update 20 Apr 2025.
- **Strength:** cheap and simple. **Weakness:** ads, a stale update history, tiny base.
- **Opportunity:** the price anchor for a single-purpose year widget is **about $5 once**: UNTIL's yearly in a high-price country should clearly add something (Life, wearables, style) to justify $14.99.

### 5 · Year Progress: Widget (iOS, Samet Pilav)
- **Positioning:** "Aesthetic time progress tools". Lock Screen wallpaper with year progress, widgets, **Apple Watch app and complications**, themes, custom colours, symbols, grid styles, **shareable progress cards**, special days. Productivity.
- **Pricing:** $0.99 Unlimited, $1.99 Yearly Premium. **Languages: English plus 18.** 5.0★ from 3 ratings; very new (Jan 2026).
- **Strengths:** low price, wide languages, customization, share cards, watch.
- **Weaknesses:** tiny rating base. **Implication:** a cheap competitor has watch, customization and 19 languages. Localization and watch are not unique to UNTIL; the *Android, desktop and life-weeks feeling* combination may be.

### 6 · Lifetime: Life Progress (iOS)
- **Positioning:** "Memento Mori, Life Time Left". Life weeks calendar free; Life Progress Tracker, widgets (Life Progress, Time Left, Quick Glance) and reminders in Pro.
- **Pricing as listed:** Pro monthly $49.99, annual $99.99, lifetime $99.99. These figures look high; the listing could show a price ladder or a regional tier. Verify before using.
- **Languages:** French, German. 4.6★ from 15 ratings.
- **Strength:** clear free/paid split with life weeks free. **Weakness:** high prices, small.
- **Opportunity:** proof that this niche tolerates high lifetime prices on iOS. Supports a $39.99 lifetime test in high-price markets.

### 7 · Life Time Left: 4K Weeks / Time Left Widgets (iOS, Edwing H Santos Mena)
- **Positioning:** life calendar plus a **Stoic journal with weekly AI reflections**, PDF export, home-screen countdown widgets.
- **Pricing:** Lifetime $19.99, Pro $2.99, Pro $9.99. English. 4.6★ from 18 ratings. Subtitle "AI Stoic Journal Calendar".
- **Strength:** extra value layer (journal/AI) for people who want to *do* something with the awareness.
- **Weakness:** on-device privacy claims aside, AI features add cost and complexity; iOS only.
- **Opportunity:** the "what do I do with the feeling" need is real. UNTIL's reflections and Ember cover a lighter version without an AI cost.

### 8 · Mori / Death Clock (iOS, Silpho)
- **Positioning:** a calm life calendar plus Stoic quotes, widgets, Live Activities; WHO life-expectancy data; **Health & Fitness** category; released Mar 2026.
- **Pricing:** weekly $4.99 and lifetime per a tracker; all revenue in-app, no ads; tracker estimate under $10K a month.
- **Store signal:** one rating of 1★.
- **Strength:** aggressive weekly pricing in a micro-niche. **Weakness:** morbid brand ("death clock").
- **Opportunity:** a **gentler brand** can own the same demand without the repel; and the weekly price shows what impulse buyers accept.

### 9 · Widgetsmith (iOS, Cross Forward)
- **Positioning:** general widget and theme platform; Lock Screen widgets, countdown timers, weather. Productivity chart rank #40 free (page).
- **Pricing:** Premium listed at $1.99, $3.99, $19.99, $29.99 (unlabeled); third-party pages show roughly $2–3 a month or $20–30 a year depending on country.
- **Rating:** 4.6★ from **779K** ratings. English.
- **Strength:** scale and brand. **Weakness:** not a time-awareness product; generic.
- **Opportunity:** the clearest pricing anchor for *widget* apps: **about $20–30 a year**. Also a content partner (widget aesthetic communities).

### 10 · Event-countdown class
- **Countdown Widget • Timer (iOS):** subtitle "Vacation, Wedding, Retirement"; unlimited countdowns, widgets, English, no data collected; **$0.99 week / $2.99 month / $14.99 year**; too new to show ratings.
- **Days Matter (Android, Clover Team):** ~2.1M installs, 4.6K ratings (tracker), ad-supported with IAP; widgets; prices not seen.
- **Countdown Widget (Android, Prometheus Interactive):** 13.1M installs, 130K ratings, 4.57★ (tracker).
- **Countdown (Sunflowr):** premium adds home-screen widgets; yearly $9.99, lifetime $19.99 in a Sept 2024 snapshot.
- **Reviews:** a complaint on a similar app says the home screen widget was locked behind subscription.
- **Implication:** this class sets the **price and paywall expectation** for any countdown feature ($10–15 a year) and shows widgets are the thing apps charge for. It is also the class UNTIL's current title ("Countdown") competes with, where installs are huge but intent is different.

### 11 · Time Timer (iOS, Android per vendor)
- **Positioning:** the original visual timer (a disk that empties). Free to download, one **$9.95** unlock; the vendor also sells subscription and enterprise editions. Mixed reviews: some features moved behind a subscription, a user asks for a lifetime option.
- **Opportunity:** UNTIL's Day ring is a close cousin for "see time pass". Adjacent audience (focus, ADHD, classroom).

### 12 · Tiimo (iOS, Android)
- **Positioning:** visual daily planner, popular with ADHD users, AI co-planner, widgets in the paid tier (third-party). Prices disagree across pages ($5.99–$12.99 a month; $47.99–$89.99 a year). Verify before use.
- **Opportunity:** proof that people pay $50–90 a year for *help with time*. UNTIL does not plan days; but "time blindness" is a stated value prop on the website `[CODE]` (`website/src/domain/copy.ts`: "Less time blindness"), and a calm Day ring serves it. Avoid health claims.

## 5. Underserved needs UNTIL can address

1. **A polished life-in-weeks experience on Android.** In my searches the life-calendar apps were iOS listings; the Android options I found were free or ad-supported utilities with 13K–23K installs. `[BENCH]`
2. **A gentle, non-morbid tone.** "Death clock" brands exist; a calm "life in weeks" is open.
3. **One purchase across phone, watch and desktop.** Single-platform apps cannot offer this.
4. **Clear pricing in a category with paywall complaints.** Reviews of a hard-paywalled competitor complain of being asked to pay after entering data and of double charges.
5. **Fair regional pricing.** Few listings show per-country thinking; most use one dollar ladder.
6. **Honest personalization from a short quiz** (UNTIL's onboarding) rather than a blank grid.
7. **Time blindness / focus audience** (adjacent), if handled without medical claims.

## 6. What UNTIL cannot claim

- *Not* the only multi-platform app: Year Progress: Widget has Apple Watch and 19 languages; Left has 30 languages.
- *Not* the only one with sharing or customization.
- *Not* private in the strict sense: analytics are on by default `[CODE]`; LifeWidget states no data collected. Say "your data stays on your device" only for what is stored locally.
